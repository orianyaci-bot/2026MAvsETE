- Family reunification:

- Seasonal programs where needed:
  - Agriculture and hospitality can use well‑regulated seasonal visas with decent housing and the option to switch employers, so no one is trapped.

- Enforcement that targets the real problem:
  - Hit wage theft and misclassification hard. Same job, same pay—period.
  - Simple, fair verification systems with protections against discrimination.

- Integration and community support:
  - Fund short job training at community colleges, ESL, and credential recognition.
  - Help schools and clinics scale up so locals and newcomers feel the system working, not cracking.
