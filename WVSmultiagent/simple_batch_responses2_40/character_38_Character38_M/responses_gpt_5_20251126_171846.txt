Fairness, bottom line, is rules you can trust, responsibility matched to opportunity, and a community that doesn’t let desperation be a business model. That’s how you balance it.

  - Language and civics orientation offered early; credential evaluation to get people working at their skill level.
  - Strong enforcement against wage theft and “under‑the‑table” schemes—big fines, real bans.
  - Universal verification for employment to cut the incentive for shadow hiring.
  - After a set period of playing by the rules—work, taxes, clean record—create an earned path to long‑term status or citizenship.

I won’t endorse complete openness or blanket refusal. We need a system that’s disciplined, humane, and honest about capacity. If we keep the standards high, the data transparent, and the welcome paired with responsibility, most people—locals and newcomers—can live with it and thrive under it.
