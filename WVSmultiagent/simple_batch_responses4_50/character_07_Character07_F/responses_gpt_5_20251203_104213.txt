- Admit based on verified job demand, with wage safeguards. Use audited shortage lists and require employers to pay above local medians for those roles. Make visas portable so workers can change jobs, and enforce labor standards so nobody wins by undercutting.

- Set numerical limits that flex with conditions. Annual caps by category—economic, family, humanitarian—with bands that adjust to unemployment and capacity (housing, school seats, clinic coverage). Review and update annually through an independent commission, and publish the rationale.
