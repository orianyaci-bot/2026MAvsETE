- Equal protections to prevent a race to the bottom. When jobs are scarce, I’m comfortable giving locals the first crack—on public contracts, require local recruitment and apprenticeships. Underneath it all is a simple ethic I learned hiring younger folks: don’t pit people against each other. Set fair terms, enforce them evenly, and grow the pool of qualified workers.
