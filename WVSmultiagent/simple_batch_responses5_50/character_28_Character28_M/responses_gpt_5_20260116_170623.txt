  - Prioritize spouses, minor kids, and caregivers within the caps. Longer chains can be slower, so we don’t outpace housing and schools.

- Expect participation and help people meet it.

Fairness to me is the same rules for everyone, compassion with boundaries, and honest accounting of what a town can carry.

Complete openness ignores the strain and invites bad actors to game the system. A flat refusal ignores reality and our values. A demand‑based system inside adjustable caps, with a humanitarian lane and strong employer accountability, gives us a throttle and a backbone. It lets us say yes where it helps, no where it harms, and “welcome—here’s how we do things” to those who arrive.
