The balance between fairness and integration? It comes when policy says: *You are welcome here—if you come through the door, we will treat you with dignity. And if we say "yes," we mean it.* Not temporary tolerance, but lasting belonging.

That’s the kind of country worth being part of. Not because it’s perfect—but because it keeps trying to align its actions with its ideals.
