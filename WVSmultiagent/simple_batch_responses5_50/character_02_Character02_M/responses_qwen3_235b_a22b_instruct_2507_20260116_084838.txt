And yeah—give undocumented people already here a real shot. Millions of us grew up with them. They pay taxes, work, go to church. Kick them out? That ain’t law and order. That’s cruelty.

At the end of the day, my policy would be: **Dignity first.** Not borders, not budgets—dignity. Because fairness isn’t about keeping people out to protect what little we have. It’s about fighting for enough for everybody.

That’s what my abuela taught me. That’s what this garden taught me. We grow better when the soil’s shared.
