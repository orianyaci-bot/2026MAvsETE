Fairness, when jobs are tight, starts with this: don’t pit worker against worker, and don’t let anyone game the system. The deal should be the same for everybody who steps onto the job—same pay for the same work, same safety rules, taxes paid, no back doors.

How I’d balance locals and migrant workers:

- Train our own. Put a slice of visa fees and fines into apprenticeships, community college programs, CDL courses—the trades that keep getting skipped. Make it easier for a 40‑ or 50‑year‑old to re-skill without losing their shirt.

Why this feels fair to me: I don’t blame the guy trying to feed his family.
