In the end, my ideal policy reflects a simple belief: **immigration should be orderly, not punitive; practical, not fearful; generous, not grudging.** It should recognize that people move—not because they hate their home countries, but because they love their children enough to seek safety, dignity, a chance.

That balance—between realism and decency—that’s what I’d fight for. Not perfection. But progress. With eyes open, and heart engaged.
