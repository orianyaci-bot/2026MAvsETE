Tying admissions to proven need, setting adjustable limits, and raising standards protects working people and makes room for newcomers to contribute without breaking the seams.

Fairness, to me, is everybody playing by the same rules, with a steady hand outstretched. That’s how you keep wages decent, neighborhoods calm, and the welcome honest.
