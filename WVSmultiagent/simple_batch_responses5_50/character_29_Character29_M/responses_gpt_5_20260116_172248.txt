- Adjustable caps: Set sector‑ and region‑specific numbers based on labor market and housing capacity, review them annually, and allow limited mid‑year adjustments if conditions change. Keep refugee numbers on a separate track so humanitarian commitments aren’t squeezed by labor caps.
