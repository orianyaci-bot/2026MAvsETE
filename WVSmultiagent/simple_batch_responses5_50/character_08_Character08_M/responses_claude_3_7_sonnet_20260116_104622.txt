Complete openness is just not realistic. No country can survive with no borders at all. We've got limited resources, limited jobs, limited housing. You can't fit the whole world in, and honestly, it wouldn't be good for America to try. We'd lose what makes us who we are.

What's fair is a system where the rules are clear and they're actually enforced. Where we welcome people who come the right way and contribute, but don't reward breaking the law. Where we recognize that being American means something special - it's not just about showing up.

Social integration happens when immigrants are spread out, not clustered in isolated communities where they never have to learn English or adapt to American ways. It happens when we expect people to become American, not just live in America.

The bottom line is we need a system that puts American citizens first while still being humane and recognizing the positive contributions legal immigrants can make. That's the balance I'd want to see.
