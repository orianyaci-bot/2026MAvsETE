For social integration, I think we need to be clearer about American values and expectations. Previous generations of immigrants understood they were expected to assimilate to some degree. Fairness means having one clear system that applies to everyone equally. No special pathways, no looking the other way for some groups but not others. The rules should be transparent, consistent, and actually enforced.
