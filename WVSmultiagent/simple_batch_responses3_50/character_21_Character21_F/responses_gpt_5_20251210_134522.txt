My bottom line is balance: welcome people in ways that meet labour needs and honour humanitarian commitments, protect wages and safety with real enforcement, and invest in the places that are doing the heavy lifting. If we do those things, most of these fears soften, and the benefits of immigration become something people can feel in everyday life—not just read about.
