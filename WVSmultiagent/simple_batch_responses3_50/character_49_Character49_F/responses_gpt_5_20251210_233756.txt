  - Make wage theft and misclassification expensive; ban repeat violators from federal contracts.

  - Fund labor inspectors and publish violations so honest employers aren’t undercut.

  - Tie arrivals to housing and school capacity; send per‑person federal funds to receiving towns for classrooms, clinics, and transit.

- It matches inflows to what towns can handle, so schools, rents, and ERs aren’t overwhelmed—reducing backlash and helping everyone settle in.

In short: targeted, capped, and enforceable pathways for work; a resourced humanitarian track; real investments in local workers and receiving communities; and tough, even‑handed enforcement. That’s the mix that, to me, balances fairness with social cohesion.
