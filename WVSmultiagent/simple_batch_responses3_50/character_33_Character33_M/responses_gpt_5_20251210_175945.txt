What I’d back:

- Demand-based work visas under numeric caps. Employers must post locally, pay at or above prevailing wages, and prove the shortage. Seasonal visas with portability so workers aren’t trapped with one bad boss.

- Overall intake calibrated to capacity. Adjust caps by housing, school staffing, unemployment, and wage trends. If a town’s stretched thin, slow the pace and invest before adding more.

- Keep lanes for refugees and genuine asylum. Strong vetting, sponsor support, quick work authorization so people can stand up on their own.

Reasoning is simple: fairness to locals means the game isn’t rigged and wages aren’t undercut; fairness to newcomers means clear lanes, humane treatment, and a real chance to join the community.
