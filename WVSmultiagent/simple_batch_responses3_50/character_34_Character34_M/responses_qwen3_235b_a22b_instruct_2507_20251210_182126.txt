Adjust intake accordingly. Transparent. Adaptive. Human.

Fairness, to me, means two things: **equal treatment under clear rules**, and **recognition of different starting points**. A Syrian doctor rebuilding her life shouldn’t be processed like a Canadian investor. And a seasonal worker feeding the country shouldn’t live in fear.

Because ultimately, fairness isn’t about protecting what we have from others. It’s about building something better—*with* others. That was always Character34_M’s belief. And after all these years in the garden, watching seeds take root in shared soil—I still think he was right.
