Third, **numerical limits**? Okay, I get it—we can’t magically house and support unlimited people overnight. Infrastructure matters. Schools, clinics, housing. Longtime residents worry about rent going up, lines at the clinic getting longer. Those concerns aren’t racist—they’re human. Because fairness, to me, means nobody’s thrown away. Not the single mom from El Salvador working double shifts. Not the disabled vet in the next trailer over who fears being forgotten. Not the kid born here who’s never left the county but dreams bigger.

So my ideal policy?  
✅ Admit workers where there’s real demand—but protect their rights like we mean it.  
✅ Keep doors open for refugees and asylum seekers—honor that legacy.  
✅ Set smart, adjustable caps based on capacity, not panic.  
✅ Fund integration hard—language, housing, schools, healthcare—for *everyone*.  
✅ And enforce labor laws so no employer can profit from exploitation.

No walls. No promises of “you first, them never.” Just honesty, dignity, and a shared table—even if we have to build a bigger one.
